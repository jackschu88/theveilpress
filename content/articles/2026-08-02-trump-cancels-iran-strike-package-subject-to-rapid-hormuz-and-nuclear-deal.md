## Trump pauses Iran strike package pending rapid Hormuz–nuclear deal

US President Donald Trump said late Saturday that he had agreed to cancel a planned attack on Iran after Tehran and other Middle Eastern governments asked him to hold off, claiming the “perimeters of a deal” had been reached. The pause, he wrote on Truth Social, is contingent on being able to “rapidly make a DEAL” that would include the “Immediate, Complete, and Total OPENING OF THE HORMUZ STRAIT” and “an end to Iran’s nuclear threat.” He added that Israel joined him in that commitment.[[1]](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-kuwait-jordan-air-base/)[[2]](https://www.timesofisrael.com/trump-says-he-and-israel-have-called-off-iran-attack-after-being-assured-deal-is-imminent/)

The statement landed after multiple outlets reported that the United States and Israel had been preparing a heavy joint campaign against Iranian energy infrastructure, with strikes possible as soon as the weekend.[[1]](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-kuwait-jordan-air-base/) It also followed a June 2026 memorandum of understanding that briefly eased the naval blockade of the strait and set a short timetable on unresolved nuclear and sanctions questions—an arrangement that later collapsed into renewed strikes and shipping disruptions.[[3]](https://www.britannica.com/event/2026-Iran-war)

What is established is Trump’s public conditionality: cancel the attack *subject to* a fast deal on Hormuz access and the nuclear file. What is not established in open reporting is a signed text, verified Iranian acceptance of nuclear constraints, or operational details of any “framework.” Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, for his part, did not present the moment as a settled bargain.

## Araghchi’s regional calls

Araghchi said he had informed Turkish, Pakistani, and Saudi counterparts that Iran would act “decisively” against any further US “aggression,” stressing readiness to defend sovereignty and territorial integrity. Middle East Eye and other outlets carried the readout from his Telegram channel.[[4]](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-recap-150)[[5]](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/irans-araghchi-touts-phone-calls-warning-decisive-response-any-us)

CNN and CBS, citing the same channel and Reuters, reported separate calls with Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan and Pakistani Field Marshal Asim Munir, with language warning against US “adventurous action” and promising a forceful or “proportionate” response. Fidan publicly noted a discussion of the “latest status of the ongoing negotiation process.” Araghchi also spoke with Saudi counterparts in the same window.[[6]](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/02/world/live-news/iran-war-trump)[[1]](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-kuwait-jordan-air-base/)

Reported Iranian framing: deterrence and readiness. Reported US framing: parameters agreed, strike cancelled if a deal is closed quickly. Those frames sit side by side; they are not the same fact.

## Riyadh’s caution

Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman spoke with Trump on Saturday and raised concerns about plans for large new strikes, seeking clarity and urging de-escalation, according to Axios and corroborating accounts from CNN and CBS citing people familiar with the matter. One source told CBS the outreach was “all about concern over the targets.”[[1]](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-kuwait-jordan-air-base/)[[7]](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202608011848)

Gulf exporters have a concrete instrument at stake: commercial transit through the Strait of Hormuz. Analysts cited by CNN noted economic exposure in the Gulf Cooperation Council states if Iranian counter-strikes follow another US escalation. That is pressure on the maritime chokepoint and energy flows—not an abstract “incentive” story.

## Parallel claims from the air war

In the same morning recap cycle, Middle East Eye noted an IRGC claim that its Aerospace Force had hit the US F-35 repair hangar and ramp at Al-Azraq (Muwaffaq Salti) Air Base in Jordan with ballistic missiles.[[4]](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-recap-150) Iranian media and IRGC statements around 30 July claimed three F-35s destroyed and three heavily damaged, framed as retaliation for a US strike on Qeshm Island. Jordan said five ballistic missiles toward the base were intercepted. US Central Command denied that any US aircraft were destroyed or damaged, stating missiles and drones were intercepted or failed to reach targets.[[8]](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/iran-claims-ballistic-missile-strike-destroyed-3-us-f-35-jets-at-jordan-air-base/4013824)[[9]](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-904137)

Treat the destruction numbers as an Iranian claim contested by Jordanian interception reports and a US denial—not as verified battle damage.

## Gaza and the wider board

Middle East Eye’s 2 August morning note also recorded overnight Israeli strikes in Gaza that, according to local reporting carried there, killed eight Palestinians including two children, and other regional items (detention raids, a fire after a drone attack in Sulaimaniyah, recovery of remains in Gaza City). Those sit outside the Hormuz–nuclear track but mark that Trump’s parallel Gaza diplomacy announcements have not halted kinetic activity on the ground.[[4]](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-recap-150)

## Where the facts stop

Trump has publicly traded a strike package for a rapid deal on full Hormuz reopening and an end to Iran’s “nuclear threat,” after regional pushback including from Riyadh. Araghchi has messaged Turkey, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia that further US aggression will meet a decisive response while talks are discussed. A signed, enforceable package—nuclear verification, sanctions mechanics, shipping rules, enforcement if either side cheats—has not been published in the reporting reviewed here. Shipping incidents in and near the strait continued into the same weekend, per British maritime authorities cited by CBS.[[1]](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-kuwait-jordan-air-base/)

Watch next: whether a text appears; whether Hormuz traffic normalizes without new tolls or attacks; whether Israel issues its own public line matching Trump’s claim of joint commitment; and whether either capital treats the pause as a ceiling or a reload.
