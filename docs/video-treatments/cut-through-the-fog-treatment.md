## Notes for production
- Keep text large, clean, and minimal — never more than 2 short lines at a time.
- Let the song do the emotional lifting.
- Use the fog metaphor heavily but don't over-explain it.
- The Companion Guide should feel like the practical tool that makes the journey possible.
